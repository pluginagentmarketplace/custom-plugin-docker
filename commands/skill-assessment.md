# /skill-assessment - Evaluate Your Knowledge

Comprehensive skill assessment across 7 learning domains.

## Assessment Types

**Quick Assessment** (10 minutes)
- 10 essential questions
- Covers fundamentals
- Identifies gaps quickly

**Deep Assessment** (30 minutes)
- 30+ questions
- Covers intermediate to advanced
- Detailed feedback

**Expert Challenge** (60 minutes)
- Complex scenarios
- System design problems
- Real-world situations
